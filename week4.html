Weekly Report

Objective: To increase household-level awareness of yoga and demonstrate simple practices that can be included in everyday routines.

Detailed Report: The team visited households and explained the value of regular, simple yoga practice. A clean and accessible location was selected for a community session after considering the available space and participants' convenience. Local officials were contacted regarding the activity. A group session included warm-up exercises, basic postures and relaxation. The team also discussed cleanliness, comfortable clothing and safe practice habits. Awareness material was shared to help participants remember the key points.

Learning Outcome: I learned how to approach households respectfully, coordinate a community activity and explain safety measures. This week showed me that regular awareness and simple guidance can motivate people to include healthy practices in their daily lives.
