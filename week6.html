Weekly Report

Objective: To extend yoga awareness to elderly residents and encourage gentle, comfortable wellness practices suited to individual needs.

Detailed Report: The team identified community groups that included elderly residents and discussed permission and comfort before beginning any activity. Gentle movements and breathing awareness were introduced at a comfortable pace. Chair-supported options were used where appropriate, and participants were encouraged not to attempt movements that caused discomfort. The team also discussed relaxation, healthy sleep routines and positive social interaction. Suggestions were recorded to help plan future awareness activities.

Learning Outcome: I learned that community activities should be inclusive and adapted to participants' comfort and abilities. I also understood the value of listening carefully, giving clear safety instructions and recording suggestions for improvement.
