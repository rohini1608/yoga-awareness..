Weekly Report

Objective: To prepare the community, organize the required arrangements and encourage people to participate in safe beginner-friendly yoga sessions.

Detailed Report: The team identified suitable community spaces and discussed the arrangements needed for conducting yoga activities. Responsibilities were shared among team members, and local representatives were contacted to discuss convenient timings. Community members were introduced to beginner-friendly yoga practices. Simple awareness material about warm-up exercises, breathing and relaxation was prepared. A guided introductory session was conducted, and feedback was collected to improve the upcoming activities.

Learning Outcome: This week improved my planning, teamwork and communication skills. I learned that a successful community programme requires a suitable location, coordination, clear instructions and consideration for participants' comfort.
