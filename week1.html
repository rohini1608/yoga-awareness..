Week 1 – Permission from Sachivalayam

Project initiation and community permission

The first stage of our Community Service Project was to obtain permission from the local Sachivalayam before beginning yoga awareness activities in the community. The team explained the purpose of the project, the proposed activities and the intended community location.

ProjectYoga Awareness and Practice
Permission authorityLocal Sachivalayam
LocationRamji Nagar, Nellore, Andhra Pradesh

Permission status

Granted

Permission was obtained from the Sachivalayam to carry out the community service activities. This approval helped the team proceed with the planned awareness programme in an organized manner and coordinate with local residents.

Purpose of obtaining permission

  • To inform the local authority about the objective of the Community Service Project.
  • To seek approval before conducting awareness and demonstration activities in the community.
  • To coordinate the programme in a respectful and organized way.
  • To ensure that residents are informed about the planned yoga awareness sessions.

Team members

  • Bandi Rohini

Project location: Patanjali Dhyana Yoga Sikshana Nilayam, Children's Park and Sai Baba Temple area, 27-4-140 opposite street, Ramji Nagar, Nellore, Andhra Pradesh 524002.

Outcome

The permission process was completed successfully. The team received approval to proceed with the project activities and gained an understanding of the importance of informing and coordinating with local authorities before conducting a community programme.
