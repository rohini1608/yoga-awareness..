Weekly Report

Objective: To create yoga awareness among students and young people through simple demonstrations and interactive activities.

Detailed Report: Students and young participants who showed interest in yoga were identified. The required permission was obtained before conducting the awareness session. Basic standing and seated postures were demonstrated, with explanations about correct posture and comfortable movement. The session also covered breathing, concentration and relaxation. Participants were encouraged to ask questions so that their doubts could be addressed. Feedback and observations were collected and organized in the weekly report.

Learning Outcome: I learned the importance of permission, clear demonstrations and two-way communication. The question-and-answer activity helped me understand participants' doubts and explain yoga concepts in a more effective way.
