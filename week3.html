Weekly Report

Objective: To introduce children to basic yoga movements and create awareness about stretching, balance, breathing and relaxation.

Detailed Report: The team interacted with children in the community and identified those interested in participating. Simple stretching and balance movements were introduced through demonstrations. The importance of breathing naturally and maintaining attention was explained. Participants were reminded not to force any posture and to practise according to their comfort. A short relaxation activity was also conducted. Observations were recorded to understand the children's response and plan suitable activities for the next sessions.

Learning Outcome: I learned how to communicate with children, demonstrate simple movements and explain basic safety rules. I also understood that yoga activities should be enjoyable, comfortable and appropriate for the age and ability of participants.
